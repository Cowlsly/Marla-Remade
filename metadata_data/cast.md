Cast to your TV and speakers
Send a video, a song, a photo or a link to any Chromecast, Google TV or Nest speaker on your Wi-Fi, with play, pause, seek and volume control.
The Cast protocol is implemented from scratch over a plain TLS socket, so there is no Google Play Services and no Cast SDK - and no storage permission either, because a file you pick is read straight from the URI you chose and served only to the device you picked.
