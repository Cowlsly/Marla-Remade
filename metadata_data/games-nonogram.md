A picture logic puzzle, with a new one every day

Fill the grid from the number clues to reveal the hidden picture. One level is
open at a time, plus a fresh daily puzzle shared by everyone on the same date.
Every puzzle is generated on device and checked to be solvable by reasoning
alone, so you never have to guess. Switch between filling and crossing out, and
long press for the other one. You get three hearts per board, and a cell that
turns out to be empty costs you one.
