Receive a mirrored phone screen on your TV
The TV half of MA Cast. Install it on an Android TV or Google TV, open it once, and the TV appears in the Cast app on your phone. The first phone to connect is shown a six-digit code; after that it pairs silently.
The picture is decoded in hardware and letterboxed to fit the panel, so the phone can send its own aspect ratio at full resolution instead of padding the frame itself. Everything happens on the local network: this app has no account, no server and no need for internet access - only the phone does.
