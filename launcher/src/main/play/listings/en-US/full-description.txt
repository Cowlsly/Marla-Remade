A home screen

A home screen replacement with a paged icon grid, a hotseat, folders, a searchable app drawer, and real home screen widgets.

Widgets are hosted directly, so anything that works on your current launcher works here — including providers that need a configuration step before they appear. Icons, folders and widgets are laid out on a cell grid whose size you choose, and the layout is kept in an encrypted database on your device.

Nothing is collected, nothing is uploaded, and there is no account. Recents and the swipe-up gesture stay with the system launcher; those need signature-level access no third-party app can have.
